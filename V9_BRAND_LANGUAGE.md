Status: **LOCKED REFERENCE FOR V9**

This file is the single source of truth for positioning, naming, tone of voice and website copy direction for Andrew Tharwat Studio V9.

No new section headline, CTA, descriptor or positioning statement should be introduced into V9 unless it is consistent with this document.

---

## 1. Brand position

**Andrew Tharwat Studio is a multidisciplinary problem-solving studio.**

It brings together safety expertise, digital systems, creative thinking, design and AI to solve real problems and build practical solutions with real impact.

The studio is **not** presented as:
- a generic personal portfolio;
- a conventional marketing agency;
- a list of disconnected freelance services;
- four unrelated businesses sitting next to each other.

The disciplines are tools. **The problem and the outcome come first.**

## 6. Voice rules

### Use
- We / Our for the studio.
- Andrew / I only inside the Founder story when a personal voice is deliberately required.
- Direct, intelligent, practical language.
- Short strong headlines supported by clear explanation.
- Problem → thinking → solution → impact framing.
- Language that connects disciplines into one outcome.

### Avoid
- Generic agency language such as “we make brands shine”, “creative agency”, “360 solutions”, or vague innovation claims.
- Service-list-first language.
- Repeating Safety / Digital / Creative / AI as if they are four independent businesses.
- Overuse of “experience”, “premium”, “world-class”, “cutting-edge” without proof.
- Making AI the identity of the studio. AI is a capability, not the idea.
- Random new slogans created during design iterations.
- Switching between “I/My” and “We/Our” without a deliberate reason.

## 8. Capabilities

The four core capabilities are fixed:

### SAFETY & HSE
**People · Risk · Systems**

Field knowledge, risk thinking, awareness, training and practical HSE systems.

### DIGITAL SOLUTIONS
**Systems · Automation · Data**

Web products, internal tools, workflows, automation and useful digital systems.

### CREATIVE & BRAND
**Identity · Design · Direction**

Brand identity, visual systems, communication and creative direction built around a clear idea.

### AI & STORYTELLING
**Innovation · Content · Possibility**

AI-assisted visual production, storytelling and intelligent creative workflows that expand what the idea can become.

Capability principle:

**Different expertise. One direction.**

A project may use one capability or several. The client should not have to decide which department owns the problem before talking to the studio.

## 12. Process

Canonical process language:

**THINK. CREATE. BUILD. IMPROVE.**

- THINK — Understand the real problem and context.
- CREATE — Shape the idea and possible direction.
- BUILD — Turn it into a useful, tangible solution.
- IMPROVE — Test, refine and strengthen the result.

---

## 13. Change-control rule

Before adding or changing public V9 copy, check:

1. Does it reinforce the studio as one multidisciplinary problem-solving system?
2. Does the problem come before the service?
3. Does it sound specific to Andrew Tharwat Studio rather than a generic agency?
4. Does it use We/Our unless it is explicitly Founder copy?
5. Is it consistent with the fixed capability names and brand lines above?
6. Is it necessary, or is it just another slogan?

If any answer is no, the copy should not be added.

---

## 14. Development rule

**Structure may evolve. Brand positioning must not drift during visual experimentation.**

Prototype copy is not allowed to become production copy automatically.

Every production-facing section must be audited against this file before V9 launch.
