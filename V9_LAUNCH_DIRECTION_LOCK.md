## Scope Rules Until Launch

1. Do not propose or build a new major system unless it is required to complete an existing journey.
2. Do not expand scope because an idea is attractive.
3. Finish each feature/card end-to-end before moving to another.
4. Every change must directly support at least one of:
   - customer journey clarity
   - trust
   - conversion
   - completion of the client journey
   - Studio OS client management
   - delivery
   - Client Portal usability
   - launch reliability/security
5. Do not change the core architecture without a concrete launch-critical reason.
6. Do not touch `main` or Production before review and explicit approval.
7. Continue all implementation and QA on `v9-unified`.
8. Non-essential ideas go to **Post-Launch Backlog**.
9. The priority is a real working path:
   **Visitor → Lead → Review → Paid Client → Case/Project → Delivery → Client Portal**
10. Before implementing a new request, ask internally:
    **Does this move us closer to Launch or the first successful real client?**
    If not, backlog it.

## Decision Standard

The number of features is not a success metric.

The launch is successful when a real client can enter the system, understand the Studio, submit a need, receive and accept a scope/price, become a paid client, be managed in Studio OS, use the Client Portal, review work, receive delivery, and reach project completion cleanly.
